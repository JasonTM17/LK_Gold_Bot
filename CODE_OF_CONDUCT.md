# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained as a personal, learning-friendly Telegram bot repository. Everyone who opens an issue, sends a pull request, or discusses the project is expected to keep the space respectful and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be polite and constructive.
- Keep bug reports focused on reproducible behavior.
- Avoid sharing secrets such as Telegram bot tokens, NextDNS API keys, OTP codes, receipts, or private account data.
- Respect third-party services and their official terms.
- Propose features that support legitimate personal use, privacy, observability, or maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Requests for credential theft, subscription bypassing, unauthorized access, or abuse of third-party APIs.
- Posting private data belonging to another person.
- Spam, phishing, or malware-related content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues, remove comments, or block accounts that violate these expectations. If a discussion becomes unsafe, move it to a private channel and remove sensitive information from the public thread.
